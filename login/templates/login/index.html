Participant Guidelines

  • How to Prepare

  • Check the date, time, and venue: Make sure you know exactly where it will be held and how long it will run.
  • Plan your travel: Know the best route so you can arrive at least 15 minutes early.

  • How to answer

  • Be honest: Share what you truly think — what was helpful, what could be better. Be specific: Instead of just rating “Average,” you can add short comments like “It was a bit fast The way the Speaker is talking, maybe slow down next time.”
  • Was the topic well explained?
  • Did the instructor make it interesting?
  • Was the system easy to join and navigate?
